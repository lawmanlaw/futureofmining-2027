From what we mine to what we make.

Three editions in, the summit has become the room where Botswana’s mining decisions are made in public.

Ministers, mine operators, financiers, suppliers and the next generation of professionals share one stage. In 2027 the conversation moves to beneficiation that creates jobs at home, new ways to finance the next wave of projects, the energy transition on site, and the skills a digital mine needs.

Every session ends in something concrete, with white papers developed alongside the Botswana Chamber of Mines and government.

Eight themes. One decade.

The 2027 agenda runs through the questions that will decide the industry’s next ten years.

    Questions, answered.

    When and where is the 2027 summit?

    In Gaborone, Botswana in June 2027. Exact dates and venue will be confirmed here and emailed to everyone registered.

    How can I pay?

    By Visa or Mastercard. Payment happens on the payment provider’s secure page, so we never see your card details. You get a receipt by email straight away.

    Is there a group rate?

    Book five or more delegate passes in one order and 10% comes off those passes automatically.

    Can I change my badge or transfer my pass?

    Yes. Email us your booking reference before the summit to update badge details or transfer a pass to a colleague at no cost.

    Is it free for students?

    Yes. Under the Young Leaders programme, full-time students attend both days free. Register online and bring a valid student card to registration.
