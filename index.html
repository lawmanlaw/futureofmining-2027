      Good questions

      Can’t find it here? Email the summit team.

      How do I use my pass?

      After you register, each person gets their badge and QR code by email. Show the QR on your phone at the registration desk in the Boipuso Hall foyer and your printed badge is handed over. Wear it all day: it is scanned at the hall doors and in Ditshupo Hall.

      Where do I park?

      Delegates park in Parking lot 2, between Boipuso Hall and Ditshupo Hall, two minutes’ walk from registration. Speakers and guests of honour use the north car park off the main road. Parking is free; marshals will direct you. Directions in Google Maps.

      Where do heavy trucks enter?

      Through the service gate on the south road, straight to Ditshupo Hall’s loading doors and the outdoor ground, never through delegate parking. Trucks and machinery move in on the two days before the summit in booked slots, emailed after you book a stand.

      When can exhibitors set up?

      Ditshupo Hall opens for build-up the day before the summit, from 07:00 to 20:00. Outdoor plots open two days before for machinery. Breakdown starts after the closing session on day two.

      Is it free for students?

      Yes. Under the Young Leaders programme, full-time students attend both days free. Register online and bring a valid student card to registration.

      How can I pay?

      By Visa or Mastercard on the payment provider’s secure page, so we never see your card details. You get a receipt by email straight away.

      Can I change my badge or transfer my pass?

      Yes. Email us your booking reference before the summit to update a badge or transfer a pass to a colleague at no cost.
